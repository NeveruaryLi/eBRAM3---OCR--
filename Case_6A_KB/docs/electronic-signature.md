- Easy to use – signs documents anywhere, anytime from any devices such as smart phones, tablets or laptops.
- Web-based portal that does not require an app installation.
- The e-signing process is tracked securely and saved in a secured environment with the document’s hash value protected by Blockchain technology to prevent file amendments.
- It meets the requirements of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance (Cap. 553) in Hong Kong.
