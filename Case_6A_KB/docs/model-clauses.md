## Avoid Disputes and Protect Your Business. Simply Click and Copy eBRAM Model Clause to Your Contracts.

Disputes often arise. Litigation could be costly and time-consuming. eBRAM’s dispute resolution clauses safeguard your business at the time you seal a deal.

Our ODR platforms offer a secure, cost-effective, fast and convenient way to resolve your disputes effectively once happen.

Simply CLICK the buttons to COPY the relevant CLAUSE below based on your need and paste the clause into your contracts for an additional protection.

### APEC ODR

– an expedited 3-stage procedure to resolve cross-border B2B disputes in around 2.5 weeks to 7 weeks.

**MODEL CLAUSE** Copy CLAUSE

Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract, shall be settled in accordance with the APEC Rules of eBRAM in force when the Notice of Claim is submitted to eBRAM (the “eBRAM APEC Rules”).

### eBRAM Arbitration

– any types of disputes regardless the amount and origin of the disputes.

“Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract, or the breach, termination or invalidity thereof, shall be settled by arbitration in accordance with the eBRAM Arbitration Rules.”

Note: The parties should consider adding:

1. The number of arbitrators shall be [one/three].
2. The place of arbitration shall be [Hong Kong SAR].
3. The applicable law of the arbitration agreement and of the procedure for arbitration under the eBRAM Arbitration Rules shall be the laws of [the Hong Kong SAR].
4. The language used in the arbitration under the eBRAM Arbitration Rules shall be [English/such language as the parties agree].

### eBRAM Mediation

– looking for an amicable and cost-effective way to resolve disputes.

“Any dispute or difference arising out of or relating to this contract shall be referred to the eBRAM International Online Dispute Resolution Centre Limited and resolved in accordance with its current Mediation Rules.”

### COVID-19 ODR Scheme

– speedy and cost-effective solution to disputes arising out the COVID-19 pandemic

“Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with the present contract and within the scope of the COVID-19 Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) Scheme shall be finally settled by ODR under the eBRAM Rules for the COVID-19 ODR Scheme.”
