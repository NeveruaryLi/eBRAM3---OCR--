### **Experience real-time machine transcription with eBRAM**

**Real-time transcription support - Get instant transcriptions of your hearings and meetings with our cutting-edge machine transcription technology.**

**Competitive pricing**

Save on hearing support costs and allocate your legal budget to where it truly matters - legal advice and advocacy.

**Dedicated hearing manager**

Focus on making legal submissions and advocacy. Leave the logistics and management of the hearing to eBRAM.

**Multilingual support**

Integrated real-time machine transcription is available in English, Chinese (Cantonese & Mandarin), Arabic, Russian, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Malaysia, Tagalog, French, German and Portuguese.

Real-time machine translation of transcription is available in English, Chinese (Traditional or Simplified).

**IT support**

Less fumbling with IT equipment - be able to speak with an IT expert whenever you need help during a hearing.

### **Additional hearing support**

**In-person hearing facilities**

Not all hearings need to be conducted remotely. We offer convenient physical venues for in-person hearings with facilities equipped with audio and video equipment.

**Optional add-on service for human polishing of machine transcript**

Human polishing of machine transcription available in English, Cantonese and Mandarin.
