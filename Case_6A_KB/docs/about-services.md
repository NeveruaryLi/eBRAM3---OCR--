**Vision**

Elevate Hong Kong as the leading hub in Online Dispute Resolution (ODR), deal-making and LawTech, and contribute to further strengthen its status as the international legal hub.

Provide a one-stop speedy and cost-effective ODR and LawTech service incorporated with state-of-the-art technology and high level of security for cross-border deal-making, dispute avoidance and dispute resolution.

**Mission**

Seek collaborations with dispute resolution institutes, legal professional bodies, and chambers of commerce in different jurisdictions to promote HK’s ODR services in the global arena and explore business opportunities for local ODR professionals.

Assist law firms, commercial enterprises and other dispute resolution institutes to enhance productivity with the aid of our state-of-the-art technology.

**Values**

**Innovation and Integrity**: It’s a part of our DNA.

**Quality**: Differentiate ourselves through the high quality of our service delivery.

**Reliability**: Strive for continual improvement in operation/ services to match with users’ expectations.

**Sustainability**: Support sustainable development by optimising resource use.
