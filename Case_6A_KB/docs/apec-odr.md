### An overview of the APEC ODR Framework

The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (“**APEC**”) Collaborative Framework for Online Dispute Resolution (“**ODR**”) of Cross-Border Business-to-Business Disputes (“**B2B**”) ("**APEC ODR Framework**") establishes an APEC-sponsored initiative to use ODR to help global businesses (in particular, micro-, small-, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs)) within the APEC economies, resolve B2B cross-border disputes focusing on low-value disputes.

**Objective**: The ODR Framework aims to promote B2B cross-border confidence by providing ODR and enforcement of disputes across borders, languages, and different legal jurisdictions.

**Procedure**: Under this APEC ODR Framework, a business may file a cross-border complaint online against a business in another participating economy in cases where both businesses have consented to have such disputes resolved under the APEC ODR Framework. During the first phase of the procedure, the businesses are allowed to exchange information and proposals and negotiate a binding settlement of their dispute through electronic means (“**Negotiation Stage**”). If the parties cannot reach a binding agreement by amicable negotiations, the relevant ODR provider will appoint a qualified neutral to mediate the dispute (and if possible, reach a binding settlement agreement) (“**Mediation Stage**”) or to arbitrate the dispute (and issue a binding award) (“**Arbitration Stage**”).

**ODR provider**: eBRAM is an ODR provider under the APEC ODR Framework. As a listed ODR provider, eBRAM has self-certified its compliance with the APEC ODR Collaborative Framework and the Model Procedural Rules.

Businesses in the APEC economies may resolve their disputes in accordance with the [APEC ODR Rules of eBRAM](https://www.ebram.org/apec_odr.html), through the [eBRAM APEC ODR platform](https://www.ebram.org/apec_odr.html).
