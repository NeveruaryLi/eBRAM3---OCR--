eBRAM was established in 2018 as a non-profit alternative dispute resolution institution. We are committed to promoting online dispute resolution through innovative technologies, leading the development of secure, efficient, and flexible platforms that offer tech-driven mediation and arbitration services.

We currently have four panels open for application for professionals with relevant background and qualifications:

1. eBRAM Panel of Mediators
2. eBRAM Panel of Arbitrators
3. Neutral under eBRAM APEC Rules
4. eBRAM Panel of Domain Name Dispute Resolution

Each panel is based respectively on our Mediation, Arbitration and APEC Rules, which are available for review on our [website](https://www.ebram.org/rules.html).

Becoming a panel member entails that if parties seek eBRAM's assistance in selecting a suitable arbitrator, mediator, or neutral for their case, eBRAM will give priority to identifying the appropriate individual from the panels. Our current lists of panel members can be viewed here: [eBRAM Panels](https://panel-form.ebram.org/en/panel?platform=odr).

The application process consists of two stages:

First, you will need to create an account on our [ODR platform](https://odr.ebram.org). You can then join our panels by clicking the "Join eBRAM Panel and Lists" button (see below).

![ODR Platform Homepage Screenshot](https://www.ebram.org/panel_application/en.png)

If you encounter any difficulties, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Upon receiving your application, the application will be submitted to our Panel Listing Committee our Panel Listing Committee for vetting and approval. We will reach out to you if further documentary evidence, information, or consent is necessary for the application.

Please be advised that the Panel Listing Committee may require some time to review the application. We appreciate your patience in advance and eagerly anticipate your application.
