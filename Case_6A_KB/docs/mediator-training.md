Background

Amid the flourishing digital economy, cross-border transactions have surged, accompanied by increasingly complex and diverse disputes. To address these challenges, eBRAM International Online Dispute Resolution Centre—Hong Kong’s sole APEC-recognized online dispute resolution (ODR) platform—has meticulously designed the eBRAM Certified Online Mediator Training Course. This program enhances certified mediators’ proficiency in legal technology, empowering them to leverage ODR platforms more effectively in cross-border dispute resolution.

Key Features

- **Expert-Led Training**：Industry specialists deliver cutting-edge content on legal tech applications and practical ODR platform skills, integrating eBRAM’s advanced technology.
- **Hands-On Simulation Exercises**：: Curriculum focuses on real-world online mediation needs, covering ODR workflows, mediation techniques, and eBRAM platform operations to ensure rapid skill acquisition.
- **Flexible Learning**：Hybrid model combines in-person classes with online simulated mediation cases, allowing self-paced learning anytime, anywhere.
- **Global Certification**：Graduates receive eBRAM certification, boosting international competitiveness.

Curriculum

**Part 1: In-Person Sessions**

- eBRAM Online Mediation Fundamentals: Introduction to eBRAM, online mediation concepts, distinctions from traditional mediation, and key considerations.
- eBRAM ODR Workflow: Step-by-step guidance on case handling, including digital identity verification (eKYC), dispute identification, stakeholder communication, and solution implementation.
- Technology Applications: Exploration of eBRAM’s blockchain and AI technologies in mediation practice.

**Part 2: Online Simulated Mediation**

Cross-Border Mediation Simulation: Group exercises simulating transnational transaction disputes to refine practical ODR platform skills.

Eligibility

Mediators accredited by the Hong Kong Mediation Accreditation Association Limited (HKMAAL).

Certification Requirements

- 100% attendance in in-person sessions;
- Completion of three assigned simulated cases on the eBRAM ODR platform by 12 May 2026.
- Graduates fulfilling both criteria receive a Certificate of Completion and may apply to join the eBRAM List of Online Mediators within six months, becoming eBRAM Certified Online Mediators.
